# FlowShield

## The goal

We live in a world built to steal our attention. Endless feeds, constant pings, one more tab. The goal behind FlowShield was simple but ambitious: help people take back control of their focus and actually finish the work that matters to them.

## The solution

We built FlowShield to meet people wherever they work — not just one app, but a complete system that follows you across every screen in your life:

- **On the web** — a full dashboard to plan focus sessions, see exactly where your time goes, set goals, and get coaching that keeps you moving.
  → [flowshield.app](https://flowshield.app)

- **On your computer (Windows desktop app)** — quietly runs in the background, tracks your focus, and blocks the apps and sites that pull you away during deep work — even when you're offline.
  → [Download](https://github.com/asifthewebguy/FlowShield/releases)

- **On Linux, with private on-device AI (next-gen desktop app)** — our newest desktop app brings a private AI coach that lives entirely on your own machine. No cloud, no sending your data anywhere. It learns how you actually spend your time and gives you a personal daily briefing: what pulled you off track, where your focus held strong, and how to make tomorrow better — all completely private.
  → Install on Arch Linux: `yay -S flowshield-bin` · [AUR page](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/flowshield-bin)

- **On your phone (Android & iOS)** — start a focus session, get gentle reminders, and stay accountable on the go.
  → _coming soon_

- **In your browser** — a live timer right in your toolbar and instant warnings the moment you drift onto a distracting site.
  → Chrome: [Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/flowshield/pjjmmmefbcmcckgmdoceapgbdnjbffdg) · Firefox: _coming soon_

Everything stays in sync. Start a session on your laptop, see it on your phone. One focus, everywhere you are.

On top of that, we added a **personal AI coach** for real, practical advice, and **team challenges and leaderboards** so focus becomes something you build together, not alone.

## The impact

People stop drowning in distractions and start finishing what they set out to do. They waste less time lost online, build better habits that actually stick, and end each day with real progress to show for it. FlowShield turns focus from a daily struggle into something that just happens — and with private, on-device AI, it does it without ever putting your data at risk.
