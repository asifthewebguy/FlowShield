# FlowShield Social Media Strategy & Content Plan

## 🎯 **Social Media Strategy Overview**

### **Goals**
- **Primary**: Build awareness for FlowShield before launch
- **Secondary**: Create engaged community of productivity enthusiasts
- **Tertiary**: Generate 1,000+ followers across platforms by launch

### **Target Audience**
- **Primary**: Knowledge workers (25-40) - developers, designers, consultants
- **Secondary**: Students (18-25) - college/university students
- **Tertiary**: Remote teams and productivity enthusiasts

### **Platform Priority**
1. **Twitter/X** - Tech community, real-time engagement
2. **LinkedIn** - Professional audience, B2B opportunities
3. **Instagram** - Visual content, lifestyle productivity
4. **TikTok** - Younger audience, viral potential
5. **YouTube** - Long-form content, tutorials
