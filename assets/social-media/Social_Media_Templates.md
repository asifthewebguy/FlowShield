## 📊 **Analytics & Tracking**

### **Key Performance Indicators**
```
Engagement Rate: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Followers
Reach: Total unique accounts reached
Impressions: Total times content was displayed  
Click-through Rate: Links clicked / Total impressions
Follower Growth: New followers / Time period
Mention Tracking: Brand mentions and sentiment
```

### **Content Performance Metrics**
```
Top-performing post types
Best posting times per platform
Most effective hashtags
Highest-engagement topics
Video completion rates
Story completion rates
```

### **Tools for Tracking**
```
Native Analytics: Each platform's built-in analytics
Buffer/Hootsuite: Cross-platform scheduling and analytics
Google Analytics: Website traffic from social media
Mention.com: Brand mention tracking
Canva: Content creation and performance tracking
```
