# FlowShield Brand Guidelines

## 🛡️ **Logo Usage**

### **Primary Logo**
- The FlowShield logo consists of a shield with flowing wave elements
- Shield represents protection from distractions
- Wave elements represent flow state and seamless sync
- Available in full color, white, and black variations

### **Logo Clear Space**
- Minimum clear space: 1x the width of the shield
- Never place text or other elements within this space
- Maintain consistency across all applications

### **Logo Don'ts**
❌ Do not stretch or distort the logo
❌ Do not use on busy backgrounds that reduce visibility
❌ Do not change the logo colors outside brand palette
❌ Do not use drop shadows or effects
❌ Do not rotate or skew the logo

## 🎨 **Color Palette**

### **Primary Colors**
```
Primary Blue: #1E88E5
- RGB: 30, 136, 229
- HSL: 207°, 80%, 51%
- Use for: Primary actions, main branding

Deep Blue: #0D47A1  
- RGB: 13, 71, 161
- HSL: 218°, 85%, 34%
- Use for: Shadows, depth, accents

Light Blue: #64B5F6
- RGB: 100, 181, 246  
- HSL: 207°, 87%, 68%
- Use for: Highlights, secondary elements
```

### **Neutral Colors**
```
Pure White: #FFFFFF
- Use for: Backgrounds, text on dark

Dark Gray: #2C2C2C
- RGB: 44, 44, 44
- Use for: Text, backgrounds

Light Gray: #F5F5F5
- RGB: 245, 245, 245
- Use for: Secondary backgrounds
```

### **Color Accessibility**
- Ensure minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for text
- Test all color combinations for accessibility
- Provide alternative text for color-coded information

## 📝 **Typography**

### **Primary Typeface: Montserrat**
- **Bold**: Headlines, titles, CTAs
- **SemiBold**: Subheadings
- **Regular**: Body text (large)

### **Secondary Typeface: Inter**
- **Medium**: UI elements, labels
- **Regular**: Body text, descriptions
- **Light**: Supporting text

### **Hierarchy**
```
H1: Montserrat Bold, 48px, #2C2C2C
H2: Montserrat SemiBold, 36px, #2C2C2C  
H3: Montserrat SemiBold, 24px, #2C2C2C
Body: Inter Regular, 16px, #2C2C2C
Caption: Inter Regular, 14px, #666666
```

## 🎯 **Brand Voice & Tone**

### **Brand Personality**
- **Protective**: We guard your most valuable asset - attention
- **Empowering**: Giving users control over their digital environment
- **Reliable**: Always there when focus is needed
- **Modern**: Tech-forward but approachable
- **Calm**: Reducing anxiety about productivity

### **Voice Characteristics**
- **Confident** but not arrogant
- **Helpful** without being condescending  
- **Clear** and jargon-free
- **Encouraging** rather than demanding
- **Professional** but friendly

### **Tone Examples**
```
✅ "Shield your flow from distractions"

✅ "Concentrate without compromise"

❌ "Stop procrastinating now!"
❌ "Ultimate productivity hack"

❌ "Revolutionary breakthrough"
```

## 📐 **Logo Specifications**

### **Minimum Sizes**
- Digital: 24px height minimum
- Print: 0.5 inch height minimum
- App icon: 1024x1024px for submission
- Favicon: 32x32px, 16x16px

### **File Formats Needed**
- **Vector**: .SVG, .AI, .EPS
- **Raster**: .PNG (transparent), .JPG
- **Web**: .SVG, .PNG (2x retina)
- **Print**: .PDF, .EPS

## 🖼️ **Visual Elements**

### **Iconography Style**
- **Stroke weight**: 2px
- **Corner radius**: 4px
- **Style**: Outlined, minimal
- **Color**: Primary blue or dark gray

### **Photography Style**
- **Clean, minimal workspaces**
- **Natural lighting preferred**
- **Focus on devices and productivity tools**
- **Avoid cluttered or distracting elements**

### **Illustration Style**
- **Geometric shapes**
- **Flow and wave elements**
- **Shield motifs**
- **Blue gradient applications**

## 📏 **Layout Principles**

### **Grid System**
- **12-column grid** for web layouts
- **8px baseline grid** for spacing
- **Consistent margins**: 24px, 48px, 96px
- **Content max-width**: 1200px

### **Spacing**
- **Micro**: 8px (UI elements)
- **Small**: 16px (related content)
- **Medium**: 32px (sections)
- **Large**: 64px (major sections)

### **Alignment**
- **Left-aligned** text for readability
- **Center-aligned** headlines and CTAs
- **Consistent margins** across all materials

## ✅ **Brand Checklist**

### **Before Publishing**
- [ ] Logo is clearly visible and properly sized
- [ ] Colors match brand palette exactly
- [ ] Typography follows hierarchy rules
- [ ] Messaging aligns with brand voice
- [ ] Clear space around logo is maintained
- [ ] Accessibility requirements are met
- [ ] All text is spell-checked and proofread

### **Quality Standards**
- [ ] High resolution (300 DPI for print, 2x for digital)
- [ ] Proper file formats for intended use
- [ ] Consistent branding across all touchpoints
- [ ] Professional appearance and execution
